## What This Analysis Does

This analysis identifies **unique droplets** across 16 z-slice images (z_0 through z_15) and determines the **maximum diameter** for each unique droplet. The key innovation is that droplets appearing in multiple z-slices are recognized as the same droplet if their (x,y) coordinates are within 10 pixels of each other.

### Example

If a droplet appears at:
- z_5: position (100, 200) with diameter 25 μm
- z_6: position (102, 198) with diameter 30 μm
- z_7: position (101, 201) with diameter 28 μm

The analysis recognizes these as the **same droplet** (because positions are within 10 pixels) and records:
- Position: (102, 198) - from the slice with max diameter
- Max Diameter: 30 μm
- Source Slide: z_6

### What This Tells Us

1. **Depth Distribution**: The overwhelming majority of droplets (56.5%) achieve their maximum diameter at z_15, suggesting:
   - Most droplets are sitting at the bottom of the imaging chamber

2. **Imaging Stack Structure**: 
   - z_0 = Top of the imaging stack
   - z_15 = Bottom of the imaging stack
   - Most droplets have their widest point near the bottom

3. **Droplet Positioning**: The fact that different droplets have maximum diameters at different z-levels indicates:
   - Droplets are positioned at various depths in the imaging volume
   - Not all droplets rest at the same level

## Technical Details

### Algorithm

The analysis uses a sophisticated clustering algorithm:

1. **Load Data**: Combine all z-slice CSV files
2. **Spatial Clustering**: Group droplets within 10 pixels of each other
3. **Iterative Expansion**: Each cluster grows by including all nearby droplets
4. **Maximum Selection**: Select the droplet with maximum diameter from each cluster
5. **Record Results**: Save position, max diameter, and source slice

### Parameters

- **Error Margin**: 10 pixels (distance threshold for considering droplets as the same)
- **Input Files**: z_0.csv through z_15.csv
- **Input Directory**: `/data3/megan_data/Jimmy/Volume_Prediction/csv/1/csv_realunit/BF imaging/`

## Questions & Answers

**Q: Why 10 pixels for the error margin?**
A: This was the user's requirement. It accounts for slight position variations of the same droplet across different z-slices due to imaging tolerances.

**Q: What if I want to see all diameters, not just the maximum?**
A: The current analysis specifically finds the maximum diameter per unique droplet. To see all measurements, you would need to look at the original CSV files.

**Q: Why do so many droplets have max diameter at z_15?**
A: Most droplets are likely sitting at the bottom of the imaging chamber, so z_15 (the bottom focal plane) captures their widest cross-section.

**Q: How do I verify the results?**
A: You can:
1. Check a few droplets manually in the original CSV files
2. Look for droplets with similar (x,y) coordinates across slices
3. Verify that the reported maximum is indeed the largest diameter for those positions
