    Algorithm:
    1. Measure gap = 20 pixels
    2. Calculate edge_width = 20/2 - 1 = 9 pixels (half-gap)
    3. Dilate Droplet B by 9px → exclusion zone for A
    4. Dilate Droplet A by 9px → exclusion zone for B
    5. Detect dark pixels only in non-excluded regions
    
    ✅ SOLUTION: Dark rings are isolated!

       - Droplet B's ring: 9px (stops short of middle)

       - Each droplet only detects its OWN dark pixels

║                          SUMMARY                                         ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

The enhanced solution uses TWO protection mechanisms:

✓ Layer 1: SPATIAL EXCLUSION
   Excludes dilated regions of neighbors (not just their interiors)
   
✓ Layer 2: HALF-GAP SIZING  
   Uses only half the gap distance for edge width

Result: Perfect isolation of dark rings, even in close-packed droplets!
